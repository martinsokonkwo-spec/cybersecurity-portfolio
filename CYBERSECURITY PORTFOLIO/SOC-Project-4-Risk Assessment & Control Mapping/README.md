# Risk Assessment and Control Mapping Using NIST CSF

## Objective
To conduct a cybersecurity risk assessment and map identified risks to appropriate security controls using the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF).

## Scenario
An organization seeks to understand its cybersecurity risks and ensure that controls are aligned with recognized security standards.

## Key Tasks Performed
- Identified assets, threats, and vulnerabilities
- Assessed risk likelihood and potential impact
- Mapped risks to NIST CSF functions and categories
- Recommended controls to mitigate identified risks

## Tools & Frameworks
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- Risk assessment methodology

## Deliverables
- Risk register
- Control mapping aligned with NIST CSF

## Outcome
This project demonstrates GRC-relevant skills, risk-based thinking, and the ability to align technical risks with governance and compliance frameworks.
